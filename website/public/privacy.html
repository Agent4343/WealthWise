Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and the financial profile information you provide (age, province, income bracket, savings amounts, risk tolerance). We do not collect your real name, SIN, bank login credentials, or any information that directly identifies your financial accounts.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your financial profile data is used solely to generate personalized financial education content, including your Money Score, retirement projections, and AI Weekly Briefs. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security policies. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Apple StoreKit — subscription management
  • OpenAI API — AI-generated brief content (your data is not used to train AI models)

5. Your Rights

You can request to view, update, or delete your data at any time by contacting us at support@wealthwise.app. Upon account deletion, all your financial profile data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

6. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that follow you across the web.

7. Children's Privacy

WealthWise is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or email. Continued use of WealthWise after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@wealthwise.app.
